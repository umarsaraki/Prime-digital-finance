At Prime Digital Finance (PDF), accessible from our official application and platform, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our global visitors and investors. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information that is collected and recorded by PDF and how we utilize it.


1. Information We Collect

When you register for an Account, we may ask for your contact information, including items such as your Full Name, Email Address, Telephone Number, and Preferred Payment Account Details (Bank Account Numbers / Crypto Wallet Addresses).


2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect in various ways, including to:

    * Provide, operate, and maintain our financial services. * Improve, personalize, and expand our daily platform operations. * Process your daily deposit and withdrawal requests securely. * Prevent fraudulent activities, illegal multi-accounting, and security breaches.

3. Data Security & Storage Protocol

Your sensitive personal credentials and transactional logs are stored using advanced end-to-end Supabase SSL encryption and local secure tokenization. We do not sell, trade, or transfer your personally identifiable information to external third parties.
